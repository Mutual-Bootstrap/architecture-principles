# Secure by Design

## Description

Services will be secure by design, accreditable and auditable, protecting information appropriately while supporting transparency and disclosure obligations.

## Rationale

Failure to protect information places people, systems and the Programme at risk. At the same time, appropriate information sharing and public transparency must be supported.

## Implications

- Adopt recognised security standards.
- Establish comprehensive security policies and controls.
- Build security considerations into service design from inception.
- Ensure services are accreditably secure and auditable.
- Maintain appropriate protections across the technology estate.
