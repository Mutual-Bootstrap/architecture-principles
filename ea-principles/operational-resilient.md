# Operationally Resilient

## Description

Services will be fault tolerant and recoverable in a manner aligned to business needs, ensuring continuity of operations.

## Rationale

Unreliable services increase programme risk, create delays and increase costs. Users expect reliable digital services and will often develop inefficient or insecure workarounds if services are unavailable.

## Implications

- Design services for resilience.
- Define recovery objectives based on business requirements.
- Balance resilience requirements against cost.
- Require service providers to deliver agreed resilience capabilities contractually.
