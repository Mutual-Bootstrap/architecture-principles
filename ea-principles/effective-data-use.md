# Effective Control and Use of Data

## Description

We will understand enterprise information and data requirements and maximise the value derived from data. Master data will remain under organisational control and data will be managed throughout its lifecycle.

## Rationale

Effective programme delivery requires trusted information sharing across internal teams and partner organisations. Uncontrolled data creation or duplication increases cost, effort and risk.

## Implications

- Understand data assets and ownership.
- Maintain appropriate access controls.
- Support transformation and reuse of data.
- Enable evidence-based workflows and decision-making.
- Ensure all use of data is lawful and compliant.
- Apply information classification correctly.
- Align designs with the organisational Data Strategy.
