# Re-use, Assemble, Buy Before Build

## Description

We will create a reusable and simplified architecture that enables flexibility through adoption of cloud-first and commodity services wherever practical.

## Rationale

With limited resources, existing capabilities and commodity solutions typically provide the fastest route to delivery, lower ownership costs and reduced implementation risk.

## Implications

- Match requirements to existing capabilities where possible.
- Reuse services already present within the estate.
- Prefer commodity cloud services over bespoke solutions.
- Ensure suppliers comply with all architectural principles.
- Enforce architectural requirements through procurement and contract management.
